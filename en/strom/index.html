Circuit puzzle

STROM STROM

“Build the circuit. Press test. Let it shine.”

Switches on the left, lamps on the right, and in between you build the circuit from wires and gates. Then the test runs step by step: the switches change, current flows visibly through your wires, and every lamp has to light exactly when it should.

Screenshots from the current version of STROM

How it works

Wire it, test it, let it shine

  1. 1

    Read the table

    The test table shows which lamp should light for which setting of the switches.

  2. 2

    Wire it up

    Build the circuit from wires, splitters and gates like NOT, AND and XOR.

  3. 3

    Run the test

    Current flows visibly, step by step. If every lamp lights exactly when it should, the level is solved.

At a glance

STROM in numbers

  • 10Worlds
  • 160Levels
  • 8Parts
  • 0Time pressure
  • Ten worlds, from the first Hook-up to Arithmetic.
  • Eight parts: wire, crossing, splitter, NOT, AND, XOR, delay and latch.
  • A test table shows, step by step, which lamp should light – and where your circuit is off.
  • Delays and latches bring in time: clocks, blinkers and small memories.
  • Every level has a checked reference circuit. Can you match it with as few parts?
