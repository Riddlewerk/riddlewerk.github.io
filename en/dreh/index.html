How it works

From starting word to target

  1. 1

    Look at the target

    The target word is at the top, your starting word below it. A WORM is to become a COW.

  2. 2

    Play your cards

    Each card changes the word: reverse it, drop the last letter, put a W first. Each card only as often as it allows.

  3. 3

    Find the order

    The same cards in a different order make a different word. Match the best solution’s move count for three stars.

At a glance

DREH in numbers

  • 16Worlds
  • 640Levels per language
  • 11Card types
  • 2Play modes
  • Sixteen themed worlds of 40 levels each – from Animals, Nature and Kitchen to Fantasy and Space.
  • Six bonus worlds from Easy to Insane – playable any time, unlocked with a purchase or three short videos.
  • Separate level catalogues in English and in German.
  • Easy: every move happens at once. Hard: you plan all your moves first, then run the plan.
  • Every level is proven solvable by the solver, and the shortest solution is known – can you match it?
  • Twelve designs and six music tracks, unlocked with coins you earn by solving levels.
