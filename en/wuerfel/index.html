Cube puzzle

WÜRFEL WÜRFEL

“Roll the cube. Pick up the colours. Stamp them down.”

A cube stands on a small board. Every move tips it over an edge onto the next cell, and its six faces travel with it. When a clean face lands on a spot of paint it picks up the colour; a stamp tile wants exactly its colour underneath. A level is solved when every spot is picked up, every stamp is set and the cube stands on the goal.

Later the boards stack up to three floors: a hole drops the cube one floor down, a trampoline throws it one floor up, and portals link cells even across floors. You turn the camera round the board in quarters, so you can see every side.

How it works

Roll, pick up, stamp

  1. 1

    Roll

    Swipe across the board, tap one of the four arrows or use the arrow keys. The cube tips over onto the next cell.

  2. 2

    Pick up

    When a clean face lands on a spot of paint, it takes the colour. Its shadow and the prints round it show which face lands where.

  3. 3

    Stamp

    A stamp tile wants its colour underneath. Once every spot is gone and every stamp is set, roll onto the goal.
