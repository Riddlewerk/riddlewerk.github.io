3D building puzzle

SCHATTEN SCHATTEN

“Build the sculpture. Turn the light. Every shadow must fit.”

In a small room you can turn, you build a sculpture out of cubes, ramps and wedges. Three lamps throw its shadows: from the front on the back wall, from the side on the left wall, from above on the floor. Above the room, a panel for each shadow shows what it should look like. The level is solved only when every shadow fits exactly.

How it works

Build, turn, match the shadows

  1. 1

    Build

    Tap a floor tile or the side of a cube and a new piece stands there. A tap turns a ramp or a wedge, a long press takes a piece away.

  2. 2

    Turn

    Swipe across the room or use the arrows to turn it a quarter at a time and see what stands at the back.

  3. 3

    Match the shadows

    The panels show what fits: grey is still missing, green fits, red is too much. Match every shadow with the fewest pieces for three stars.
