Light puzzle

SPIEGEL SPIEGEL

“Bend the light. Mix the colours. Hit every target.”

Lasers send coloured beams across the board, and every target wants exactly one colour. In between you place mirrors, splitters, filters, prisms and shutters. The light follows at once: every change shows you straight away where the beams run now.

How it works

Place, turn, let it shine

  1. 1

    Read the targets

    Three dots on each target show which primary colours it needs – or a stroke that it must stay dark.

  2. 2

    Place the optics

    Pick a part, tap a cell, tap again to turn it. The light follows every change at once.

  3. 3

    Light it all

    When every target gets exactly its colour, the level is solved. Match the reference’s part count for three stars.

At a glance

SPIEGEL in numbers

  • 10Worlds
  • 160Levels
  • 5Kinds of optics
  • 0Time pressure
  • Ten worlds, from the first Beam to Sun with four lasers and every kind of optic.
  • Five kinds of optics: mirror, splitter, filter, prism and shutter.
  • Light mixes: red and green make yellow, all three make white. Some targets must stay dark.
  • No time pressure and no luck: the light follows fixed rules and shows you every change at once.
  • Every level has a checked reference solution. Can you match it with as few parts?
