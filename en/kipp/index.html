Tilt puzzle

KIPP KIPP

“Tilt the board. Every marble rolls. Each into its hole.”

A small board holds marbles in four colours and holes in the same colours. You tilt the board up, down, left or right, and every marble rolls at once, each until something stops it: a wall, the edge, another marble. The level is solved when every marble lies in the hole of its colour. If one rolls into another colour’s hole, it is gone, and you take the move back.

How it works

Tilt, roll, sink them

  1. 1

    Tilt

    Swipe the board or tap one of the four arrows. The board tilts that way.

  2. 2

    Roll

    Every marble rolls at once, cell by cell, until something stops it. Another marble stops it too, and that is often the trick.

  3. 3

    Sink them

    Each marble wants the hole of its colour. You can always take a move back, and the best score earns three stars.

At a glance

KIPP in numbers

  • 16Worlds
  • 256Levels
  • 4Colours
  • 0Time pressure
  • 16 worlds, from the first marble through walls, glue and stones to the master.
  • Deflectors send marbles round the corner, tubes carry them under walls.
  • A switch holds the gate open as long as a marble or a stone lies on it.
  • Paint pots recolour marbles, colour gates let only their colour pass, crumbling floor carries just once.
  • A proven best score for every level: the fewest moves earn three stars.
  • No luck, no time pressure: every tilt rolls exactly like that, every time.
